Institutional Capital Risk Partners

Incorporated in Dublin, Ireland, SMR Capital Ltd operates as a private, highly structured claims management vehicle and corporate risk partnership. We specialize exclusively in forensic payroll transparency audits, the legal assignment of cross-border debts, and the deployment of private liquidity reserves to resolve systemic structural liabilities within the maritime sector.

Unlike standard civil agencies, SMR Capital Ltd does not act as a traditional public advisory service or labor union. We interface with the market purely as an institutional asset acquirer under the strict provisions of Section 28(6) of the Supreme Court of Judicature (Ireland) Act 1877. This statutory mechanism permits us to take complete legal and beneficial assignment of accrued corporate wage debts, positioning our entity as the exclusive sole plaintiff in subsequent out-of-court arbitrations and civil court litigations.
