Based in Dublin, Ireland, SMR Capital Ltd operates as a private corporate vehicle specialized in the audit, contractual assignment, and structured recovery of historical cross-border maritime liabilities and statutory wage deficits.

Operations & Compliance

Our focus centers exclusively on the enforcement of territorial employment rights, compliance verification under the Maritime Labour Convention (MLC), and resolving wage structures within internal and territorial waters. We fund all litigation and administrative outlays entirely through internal private capital reserves, operating on a strictly commercial basis.

SMR Capital Ltd handles matters through private out-of-court arbitrations, corporate rectifications, and formal civil actions within the Irish Circuit and High Court jurisdictions.
