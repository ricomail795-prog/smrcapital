For Seafarers

Understand what you may actually be owed.

SMR reviews employment agreements, payment history, work patterns and supporting evidence to reconstruct potential wage and employment-related financial shortfalls.

Evidence we may review

Seafarer employment agreements, payslips, bank records, cash payment records, rosters, hours of work/rest records, vessel details, travel receipts, messages and other relevant evidence.

Every figure is classified as verified, calculated or disputed before being presented as part of a case.

For Employers & Operators

SMR's purpose is to identify and reconcile genuine financial discrepancies rather than inflate claims.

Employers should have an opportunity to provide relevant contracts, payroll records, rosters and other documents before unresolved items are escalated.

Structured Resolution

Resolve historic wage exposure before it becomes a larger dispute.

Evidence-led reconciliation can provide a more efficient route for both workers and operators where legitimate historic liabilities exist.

How It Works

A five-stage claims process.

Commercial, statutory and regulatory issues are separated so each matter follows the appropriate route.

STAGE 01

Intake

Confidential worker or employer case information and consent.

STAGE 02

Evidence

Contracts, payments, hours, expenses and vessel documentation.

STAGE 03

Audit

Forensic reconstruction and itemised financial claim ledger.

STAGE 04

Resolution

Structured commercial reconciliation where appropriate.

STAGE 05

Escalation

Independent legal or regulatory route where required.

Capital-Funded Recovery Model

Designed to remove the upfront cost barrier.

SMR Capital is being developed around privately funded case assessment and claims administration. Final remuneration structures remain subject to Irish legal and tax validation before operational deployment.

Three-Track Architecture

Track A — Accrued Receivable
Verified or calculable contractual money potentially due.

Track B — Statutory / Contentious Claim
Issues requiring statutory processes, legal interpretation or adjudication.

Track C — Regulatory Matter
Potential maritime or employment compliance matters recorded separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions.

Is the initial review free?

Yes. The intended model provides an initial confidential assessment without an upfront assessment charge.

Does the estimator show exactly what a worker is owed?

No. It is an indicative calculator. A proper case requires evidence and analysis of the applicable contractual and legal framework.

Can SMR bring every case to court itself?

No such representation is made. The correct claimant, assignment position and procedure depend on the nature of the right involved. Formal legal proceedings are referred through appropriately qualified legal practitioners where required.

What evidence should be kept?

Employment agreements, payslips, bank records, hours records, rosters, travel receipts, vessel information, messages and other evidence relevant to the employment arrangement.
