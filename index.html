Maritime Compliance

Commercial recovery and regulatory compliance are kept separate.

Maritime employment can involve domestic employment law, seafarer employment agreements, flag-state requirements, port-state oversight and the Maritime Labour Convention framework.

SMR records relevant regulatory indicators as part of the evidence file, but a commercial payment is not treated as consideration for withholding a legitimate regulatory report.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions.

Does SMR Capital charge a worker to review a potential case?

The intended model provides a preliminary assessment without an upfront assessment charge. Any later commercial terms would be clearly disclosed and agreed before proceeding.

Does the estimator tell me exactly what I am owed?

No. It is an indicative arithmetic tool only. A real case requires contracts, payment records, hours, employment dates and analysis of the applicable legal framework.

Can SMR take an employer to court?

That depends on the legal nature of the underlying right, whether it is lawfully assignable and the appropriate procedure. Formal legal proceedings and contentious legal services are handled through appropriately qualified legal practitioners where required.

What evidence should a seafarer keep?

Employment agreements, payslips, bank records, rosters, hours of work/rest records, vessel details, travel receipts, messages, photographs and any written information describing the agreed wage or working arrangement.

Will SMR report an employer to regulators?

Commercial recovery and regulatory matters are treated separately. Regulatory action depends on the facts, applicable obligations and appropriate professional or legal advice.
