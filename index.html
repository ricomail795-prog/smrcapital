1. Core Operational Focus

SMR Capital Ltd does not offer public consulting, retail legal advice, or open-market representation. Our portfolio is restricted exclusively to high-stakes, contractually assigned corporate liabilities under the following enforcement pathways:

  • **Forensic Wage Audits:** Quantitative verification of historical payroll discrepancies against territorial statutory baselines, including the National Minimum Wage Act 2000 and the Organisation of Working Time Act 1997.
  • **MLC Compliance Enforcement:** Financial rectification of structural shipowner failures under the Maritime Labour Convention (MLC 2006), specifically targeting absolute repatriation flight defaults, mandatory health and welfare liabilities, and sub-standard crew accommodations.
  • **Absolute Debt Assignment:** The total legal and beneficial acquisition of accrued employment debts under Section 28(6) of the Supreme Court of Judicature (Ireland) Act 1877, allowing our entity to pursue recovery as the sole plaintiff in its own corporate name.

2. Litigation & Settlement Protocols

Our operations prioritize the swift, non-public resolution of outstanding corporate liabilities via structured out-of-court arbitrations, tripartite compromise agreements, and absolute confidentiality protocols. By deploying extensive independent capital reserves, our board is fully prepared to execute advanced civil enforcement paths directly through the Circuit and High Court jurisdictions of Ireland should an unrectified liability structure be challenged.
